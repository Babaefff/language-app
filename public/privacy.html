Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Hablo (habloso.netlify.app) is a free website for learning Spanish, German, French and Russian, run by an individual developer. This page explains, in plain words, what data the app uses and why. In short: no ads, no tracking, no selling of data.

Without an account

You can use everything without signing in. Your progress and settings are then stored only in your own browser (its local storage). They are not sent to us, and they disappear if you clear your browser data.

With an account (optional)

Your email addressTo sign you in (with an emailed link or with Google) and to recognise your account.
Your learning progressWords, lessons, scores and XP for each language, so you can continue on another device. This includes a bounded mistake notebook and first-attempt outcomes and timestamps for delayed recall and sentence checks. We do not store what you typed in those checks.
Google profile detailsIf you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email and profile picture with our sign-in provider. We use the email to identify your account and show your name only to you, on your own profile page. We never share any of it.
Technical sign-in dataAn account ID and the times you signed in, kept by the sign-in provider for security.

We never ask for a password, and we never use your data for advertising.

Services we use

  • Supabase: stores accounts and saved progress, and sends the sign-in emails.
  • Netlify: hosts the website. Like every web host, it briefly keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and operation.
  • Google: only if you choose “Continue with Google” to sign in.
  • YouTube (Google): songs can show an embedded YouTube video. When you play one, YouTube may collect data and set cookies under Google’s privacy policy.
  • Your browser’s speech services: the Speaking practice uses your browser’s built-in speech recognition. Depending on the browser, your voice may be processed by the browser maker (for example Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari). Hablo never records or stores your voice.

The recorded word and sentence audio in the app is generated in advance and contains no personal data.

Cookies

Hablo sets no advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser’s local storage for your progress, settings and (if you sign in) your sign-in session. Embedded YouTube videos may set their own cookies when played.

How long we keep data

Account data is kept until you delete it. You can do that yourself at any time.

Your rights and choices

  • Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete account removes your email and all saved progress from our servers immediately.
  • Delete only the cloud copy: Delete cloud data removes your saved progress but keeps the account.
  • Export your progress: Settings → Export backup downloads a copy of your progress.

If you live in the EU/EEA or the UK, you also have the right to access, correct and object to the processing of your data, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Hablo is meant for teenagers and adults. If you are under 16, please ask a parent or guardian before creating an account.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
